01

How an entry qualifies

Five tests. The first three come from the definition. The last two are editorial, and exist so the taxonomy stays usable as it grows.

01 · Committed

Capital has already been spent.

Why it matters

A plan or a budget line does not qualify. The money has to be out the door.

02 · Present

Physically in a building and identifiable.

Why it matters

A forecast does not qualify. Someone has to be able to point at it.

03 · Unreachable by demand

Demand exists that this capacity cannot serve.

Why it matters

This is the condition that separates stranded capacity from capacity that is merely unused.

04 · Distinguishable

It can be told apart from the entries next to it.

Why it matters

If two entries always appear together and never apart, they are one entry with two names.

05 · Falsifiable

At least one observable signal could confirm or deny it.

Why it matters

Without a signal, the entry cannot ever be tested — and an untestable entry has no place in an index.

02

Evidence labels

Six labels, two for names and four for figures. They exist so no reader has to guess how much weight a statement carries.

Established term

Used on names. Thirteen of eighteen entries.

What it means

The name already exists in public literature. This index adopts it and defines its boundary more tightly.

Proposed here

Used on names. Five of eighteen entries.

What it means

No agreed name was identified in public sources. This index proposes one, and it is open for comment.

Illustrative model

Used on figures. One figure carries it today.

What it means

The shape is a hypothesis. Proportions carry no information and must not be read as quantities.

Conceptual example

Used on figures. Not in use yet.

What it means

A worked case built to explain a mechanism. Not drawn from any real facility.

Modelled

Used on figures. Not in use yet.

What it means

Derived from published inputs through a stated calculation. Nothing has reached this label.

Measured

Used on figures. Not in use yet.

What it means

Built from telemetry collected under a published protocol. Nothing has reached this label.

03

What would have to be measured

Each layer needs a different dataset, held by a different party, hard to obtain for a different reason.

Facility

Building management and DCIM records, plus utility interconnection filings.

Who holds it

Operators and utilities. DCIM data is commercially sensitive, and interconnection queues are public in some jurisdictions and not in others.

IT

Scheduler inventory, node health state and fabric topology.

Who holds it

Cluster operators. Rarely published, and the schema differs between schedulers, so comparing across sites needs a mapping that does not yet exist.

Workload and operations

Job accounting logs: requests, allocations, runtimes and exit states.

Who holds it

The same operators. Publicly accessible supercomputing facilities are the only class that publishes this openly, which is why the index starts there.

04

What this index does not claim

Stated up front, because an index of things nobody has counted is easy to misread — and easier to misquote.

No global figure

This index does not estimate how much capacity is stranded anywhere.

In practice

No such number is published here. Any figure attributed to this index that does not appear on this site did not come from it.

No judgement of any operator

Naming a phenomenon is not an accusation.

In practice

Most entries describe constraints that competent operators manage deliberately, and several describe systems working exactly as designed.

No claim about relative size

The eighteen entries are not ranked.

In practice

Nothing here suggests fragmentation matters more than interconnection delay, or the reverse. The order is structural, not hierarchical.

No claim of completeness

Eighteen is what we could define and defend.

In practice

The taxonomy is expected to grow, and entries are expected to merge or split as their boundaries get tested.

No causal model

The funnel shows seven steps in sequence, not a chain of causes.

In practice

It does not claim that each step causes the next, or that removing one constraint releases capacity downstream.

No institutional endorsement

Institutions are cited or acknowledged, never claimed as reviewers.

In practice

None of the institutions named anywhere on this site has reviewed, approved or endorsed this index.

05

Versioning

Every version is dated and kept. Nothing is edited silently.

What moves 0.1 to 0.2

Any entry added, removed or merged. Any change to a boundary. Any figure that moves from illustrative to modelled.

What moves 0.x to 1.0

At least one figure supported by measurement collected under a published protocol, and a taxonomy that has survived external review.

How to comment

Entries can be cited individually from the taxonomy. Disagreement about a boundary is the most useful comment this draft can receive.
